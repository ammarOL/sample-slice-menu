# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Restaurant guests browsing a digital cafe menu and building an order before checkout. They are likely on mobile or a small tablet, comparing items quickly and checking the running cost before placing an order.

## Product Purpose

AirMenus Slice is a lightweight ordering interface for a cafe menu. It should make item selection, quantity changes, checkout review, and order placement feel clear and low-friction.

## Brand Personality

Warm, direct, practical. The experience should feel like a calm cafe counter: easy to scan, modestly polished, and focused on helping the guest finish an order.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, decorative restaurant landing-page tropes, over-styled cards, and checkout flows that hide quantities or totals behind extra steps.

## Design Principles

Keep the order visible and accountable.
Use familiar controls for quantity and checkout actions.
Favor readable menu information over decoration.
Make totals and next steps obvious at the moment of decision.
Preserve a mobile-first path through browsing, review, and placing an order.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic buttons and links for interactive controls, visible focus states, readable contrast, and layouts that work on narrow mobile screens. Motion should be minimal and respect reduced-motion preferences.
